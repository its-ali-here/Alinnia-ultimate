# 🛡️ Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 27, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 27, 2026  

**Alinnia** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and website collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our meal planning, dietary management, and grocery estimation services.

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Account & Authentication Information:** When you register or sign in, we collect your email address and authentication credentials via Supabase.
- **Household & Dietary Preferences:** Dietary requirements (e.g., diabetic-friendly, high protein, allergies, low-GI requirements), household size (adults/children), preferred cuisines (Pakistani, Middle Eastern, Mediterranean, etc.), spice level, and cooking habits.
- **Meal Logs & Planning Data:** Dishes selected/approved/rejected in Dish Decider, weekly planned dinners, custom foods, notes, and grocery checklist items.
- **Device & Usage Diagnostics:** Non-identifying device information and crash reports to optimize app speed and reliability.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
- To generate personalized, culturally tailored dinner suggestions and weekly meal plans.
- To compute accurate grocery shopping quantities scaled to your household size.
- To calculate estimated grocery and recipe costs (e.g., Foodpanda PKR baselines).
- To synchronize your saved recipes, pantry items, and preferences across your devices.
- To process subscription statuses and verify in-app purchases.

### 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
- **We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal health, dietary, or profile data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.**
- We only share data with essential infrastructure providers necessary to run the app:
  - **Supabase / PostgreSQL:** For encrypted cloud database hosting and user authentication.
  - **Apple App Store & Google Play:** For secure subscription processing and payment fulfillment.
  - **Expo / EAS:** For delivering over-the-air updates and crash reporting.

### 4. Data Retention & Account Deletion
You maintain full control of your data. You can delete your account, household profiles, and all associated meal logs directly within the app at any time (**Household / Settings ➔ Delete Account**). All personal data is permanently wiped from our database upon request.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Alinnia is intended for general audiences and household meal planners aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.
